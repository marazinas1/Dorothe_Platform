What it costs

The valuation is free and without obligation for owners considering a sale. Commission is only due when your property is sold.

The commission is agreed in writing before you sign anything. For houses and apartments sold to private buyers, the law limits the buyer's share to half, so the commission is shared between buyer and seller.

Questions owners ask

How long does a sale take?

Most properties in the Saarland sell within three to six months of publication. The valuation and preparation usually take two to four weeks before that.

Do I need an energy certificate?

Yes. Its key data must appear in every property advert. If you do not have a valid one, I arrange it as part of the preparation.

Can I stay in the house during the sale?

Yes. Viewings are scheduled with you, usually in groups, so you are not disturbed more than necessary.

What if I decide not to sell after the valuation?

Then you keep the written valuation and owe nothing. Many owners use it to plan the following years.

An inherited property, settled calmly.

An inheritance raises questions nobody prepares for. What is the property worth? Which figure does the tax office want to see? And what applies when several heirs have to decide together?

I take on the part that concerns the property: valuing it, putting it in context and, if you decide to, selling it.

Market value appraisal

For the tax office what counts is the market value on the date of death, not today's asking price. Where the tax office's standardised valuation sits above the actual value, a qualified appraisal can be the basis for a correction.

I prepare the valuation using recognised methods and document it so it can be followed. The tax assessment itself is something you discuss with your tax adviser.

Co-heirs

When several heirs have to agree, a sale rarely fails because of the market. It fails because everyone has a different figure in mind. A neutral, evidenced valuation creates one number that can be discussed calmly.

From there I handle the sale so that everyone involved works from the same information, without taking sides.

What a house in the Saarland is worth in 2026

By Dorothe Waltner, 14 September 2026, 6 min read

Most owners start with an online calculator. It is a reasonable first look, but it cannot see the things that move a price most: the condition of the roof and heating, the light in the rooms, or the street itself.

Comparable sales come first

A valuation starts with what similar properties nearby have actually sold for, not what they were advertised at. Asking prices are wishes; sale prices are the market.

Condition and energy class

Since buyers must see the energy class in every advert, a poor class now shows up directly in offers. Recent work on windows, insulation or heating often matters more than the kitchen.

A valuation on site brings these together into a range you can plan with, whether you sell this year or later.
